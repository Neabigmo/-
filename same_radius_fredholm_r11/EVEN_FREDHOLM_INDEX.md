# Even Fredholm index

Under `w=z^2`, the even Wiener space is identified with a positive-power
Wiener space in `w`.  If the even symbol is

```text
Dtilde(w) = product_j (w-w_j)^(m_j) * U(w),
```

with `U` zero-free on the closed disk, multiplication by `U` is invertible;
multiplication by the polynomial factor is injective with cokernel dimension
`sum_j m_j`.  Hence its Fredholm index is `-sum_j m_j`.

A pair of zeros `+zeta,-zeta` of an even `D_R(z)` becomes the single zero
`w=zeta^2` with the same multiplicity.  It is not doubled.  Compact
perturbation invariance applies only after the actual same-radius
`L_R=M_(D_R)+K_R` theorem is established.

A certified Stage-7 lemma that a non-Gaussian entire `R` has a finite zero of
`D_R` yields only a strictly negative even index after choosing a suitable
radius.  It is not Gaussian rigidity and does not identify cokernel
functionals after compact perturbation.
