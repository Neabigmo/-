# Same-radius compactness bookkeeping

The strict map `W_R -> W_rho` with `rho<R` is compact, but that fact alone
cannot identify a noncompact principal part: bounded multiplication is also
compact after the radius loss.  R11 therefore separates the same-radius
operator `M_(D_R)` from a remainder.

For each fixed low shift, the exact fixed-band replay gives a weighted shift
whose diagonal difference tends to zero.  Its finite truncations converge in
operator norm, so that finite-band difference is compact on the same Wiener
space.  The high-shift tail is bounded by the uniform dominant estimate and
the ordinary convolution tail.  In the nondominant region, entire membership
at a larger radius supplies the factor `q^m`, `q<1`; fixed `m` leaves only
finitely many input indices and is finite rank.

The audit certifies this decomposition as bookkeeping plus finite replays.
It does not claim that the actual Stage-7 all-degree kernel has been imported
unless its formula and the uniform passage are available.  Therefore the
default R11 decision remains conservative.
