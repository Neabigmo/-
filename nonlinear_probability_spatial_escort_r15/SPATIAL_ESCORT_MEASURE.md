# Spatial escort measure and Stein identity

Let K_q=log F_q and

    d nu_q(x)=F_q(x)^3 d gamma_{q/3}(x).

The common-shift identity at z=0 makes nu_q a probability measure. For
integrable f, Gaussian integration by parts gives

    E_nu f'(X)
      = E_nu[(3X/q-3K_q'(X)) f(X)].

This is exact, not a coercive estimate.
