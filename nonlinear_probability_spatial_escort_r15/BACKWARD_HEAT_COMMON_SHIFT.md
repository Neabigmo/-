# Backward-heat common shift

Put q=t/(1+t), d=1-q, and

    F_q(s)=d^(-1/2) exp(-s^2/(2d)) L_t(s/d).

The exact positive common-shift identity is

    E_{X~N(0,q/3)} < prod_j F_q(X+d z alpha_j(theta)) >_theta = 1.

The right side is 1. An apparent exp(d z^2/2) factor is cancelled by
the explicit Gaussian factors in F_q; this is also forced by the Gaussian
benchmark F_q=1. At z=0 this is Stage19's normalization
E F_q(X)^3=1.
