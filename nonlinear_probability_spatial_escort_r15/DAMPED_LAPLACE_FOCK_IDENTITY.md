# Damped Laplace Fock identity

For L_t(y)=E exp(yX-tX^2/2), t>=0, and
Q=sum_j X_j^2-(sum_j X_j)^2/3, Hubbard--Stratonovich gives

    E_G <prod_j L_t(sqrt(t/3)G+z alpha_j)>_theta
      = E <exp(-t Q/2 + z sum_j alpha_j X_j)>_theta.

The target Q ~ chi^2_2 gives the exact identity

    E_G <prod_j L_t(sqrt(t/3)G+z alpha_j)>_theta
      = (1+t)^(-1) exp(z^2/(2(1+t))).
