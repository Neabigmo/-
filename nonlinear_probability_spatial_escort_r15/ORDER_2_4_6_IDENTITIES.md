# Orders 2, 4, and 6

Differentiating the common-shift identity at z=0, under nu_q, gives

    E K_q'' = 0,
    E[1/2 K_q'''' + 3(K_q'')^2] = 0,
    E[5/18 K_q^(6) + 15/2 K_q''''K_q''
      + 5/6 (K_q''')^2 + 15(K_q'')^3] = 0.

The last identity may be scaled to

    E[K_q^(6)+27K_q''''K_q''+3(K_q''')^2+54(K_q'')^3] = 0.

The term 3 E(K_q''')^2 is a genuine nonnegative square, but it is not
isolated because the other terms are signed.
