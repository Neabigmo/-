# Probability-only countermodel sanity

The audit records genuine non-Gaussian probability laws to guard against
overstrong probability-only claims:

* symmetric Bernoulli: L_t(y)=exp(-t/2) cosh(y);
* heat-smoothed symmetric Bernoulli;
* masses 1/6,2/3,1/6 at -sqrt(3),0,sqrt(3).

They do not claim to satisfy the full Fock identity. They only show that
real-axis MGF positivity alone cannot imply Gaussianity.
