# Overlap audit with R2/R3/R4

* R2: the posterior-variance identity is the same escort/conditional
  variance mechanism after t <-> q; R15 adds angular common-shift
  derivatives but supplies no missing coercivity.
* R3: the probability representation and posterior variance use the same
  Gaussian-channel objects, but R15 does not replace the R3 semigroup/tail
  argument.
* R4: the angular Fisher budget is a first-derivative budget. The R15
  sixth-order square is different and does not force the odd mode to vanish.

The combined common-shift hierarchy is new, while its variance interpretation
overlaps R2/R3. The rigidity closure remains open.
