# Coercivity audit

The order-six formula contains the positive quantity

    3 E_nu[(K_q''')^2] = 3 d^(-4) E_nu[(V_q')^2].

It is not a sum-of-squares identity. The K^(6), K''''K'', and (K'')^3
terms are signed; no valid boundary estimate or sign inequality was
established here. Thus

    V>=0 + mean(V)=d + order 4/6 identities => E(V')^2=0

is not proved.
