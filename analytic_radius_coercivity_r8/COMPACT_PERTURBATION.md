# Compact perturbation statement

Under the positive-radius hypothesis and the Hellinger estimate, the
two-high and three-high pieces have norm tending to zero as `N -> infinity`
from `H_R` to `H_r`.  Since `H_R -> H_r` is compact, a bounded operator whose
tails obey this radius-loss estimate is compact after the finite-band/
principal part is separated.

This is a genuine infinite-dimensional mechanism, not a claim that a finite
truncation is compact.  What is certified here is the exact tail estimate and
the compact diagonal inclusion.  Identifying the complete even Fock operator
with the precise Stage-7 Toeplitz principal part remains a separate
normalization and convergence obligation.
