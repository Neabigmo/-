# R6 decision

## B — BACKWARD_HEAT_SIGN_TRANSFER_FAILS_AT_CURRENT_INTERFACE

The parity Hadamard lemma and the interior square-root multinomial
normalization are exact local results.  The Hermite/Mehler transform is also
exact.  What is not certified is the implication from backward-heat
positivity to the pointwise inequalities needed by the Stage-8 continuum
parity kernel.  The missing item is a uniform double-scaling identification,
including the sign/location normalization and `ell^2` tail tightness.

This is a proof-interface gap, not a counterexample to the original Gaussian
characterization problem.  No global conclusion is claimed by this package.
