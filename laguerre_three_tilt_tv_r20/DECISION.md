# R20 decision

The proposed three-tilt variance inequality is **not certified**.

Exact local averaging of the available Fock circle identity permits a formal
nonzero cubic jet with `h6 = -3*h3^2/20`; after setting the unconstrained
`h5` jet to zero, the transverse Laplacian has a negative quartic leading term.
This is not an actual probability-law counterexample, so it does not disprove
the full chi-square rigidity theorem.  It does prove that the circle-average
identity, at the currently available level, cannot by itself imply (TV).

The generic Rademacher calculation independently shows that (TV) does not
follow from log-mgf convexity or ordinary tilted-variance positivity.

Recommended next direction: stop treating (TV) as the default gate and seek a
new cross-parameter/spatial identity, or prove that the complete Fock system
eliminates the formal jet by an all-orders argument.
