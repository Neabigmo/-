# Exact defect map

Let

```text
L_e=L_R^ee=M_D+K_e,
B_o=L_R^eo=M_C+K_o.
```

After R11, `K_e` and `K_o` are compact on the same radius.  Since `L_e` is
Fredholm, the exact obstruction is the finite-dimensional map

```text
Delta_rho: Y_rho --B_o--> X_rho --> coker(L_e).
```

Principal jet interpolation proves the corresponding map for
`M_D`/`M_C` is surjective whenever `Dtilde` and `Ctilde` have no common
interior zero.  It does not, by itself, prove that `Delta_rho` is surjective
after both compact perturbations.  No numerical determinant search is used
here.  Establishing or disproving this exact surjectivity is the single
remaining R12 gap.
