# Conditional Q moments

With conditional central moments `m_k` and `V=m_2`, direct iid expansion
gives the four formulas specified in the plan.  The script expands `Q` in
three independent centered symbols and applies the moment functional to each
monomial; every residual is exactly zero.

These are conditional identities.  The target values after escort averaging
are the chi-square moments `2^k k! a_t^k` and are not inferred from a finite
moment relaxation alone.
