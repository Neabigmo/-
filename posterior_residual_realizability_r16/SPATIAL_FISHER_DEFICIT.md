# Spatial Fisher deficit

Stein and the conditional triple law give `Var_nu(X) = q/3 + q^2 Var_nu(K')` and `Var_nu(X) = qd/3 + q^2 Var_Pq(ubar)`.

Therefore `Var_Pq(ubar)=1/3+Var_nu(K') >= 1/3`, with equality only in the Gaussian case. This identity is exact but does not itself close the cross-`x` coherence gap.
