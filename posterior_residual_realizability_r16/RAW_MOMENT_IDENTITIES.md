# Raw moment identities

The order-four relation is

`E_nu[(mu4-3V^2)/2 + 3(V-d)^2] = 0`.

Together with `E_nu[V]=d`, this yields `E_nu[mu4+3V^2]=6d^2`.

The order-six relation is

`E_nu[mu6+12Vmu4-7mu3^2+3V^3]=54d^3`.

The coefficient of `mu3^2` is `-7`; a positive derivative-square term is not by itself a posterior-skewness penalty.
