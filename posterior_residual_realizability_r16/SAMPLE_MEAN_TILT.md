# Sample-mean tilt

Let `P_t` be proportional to `exp(-tQ/2) mu^3` and `W(t)=Var_Pt(Xbar)`. The audited derivatives are `W(0)=1/3`, `W'(0)=-(mu4-3)/9`, and, after `mu4=3` and `mu6=15+7mu3^2`, `W'(0)=0`, `W''(0)=mu3^2/9`.

Thus a universal assertion `W(t)<=1/3` is not a valid proof route when skewness is nonzero.
