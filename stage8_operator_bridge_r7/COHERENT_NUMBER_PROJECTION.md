# Number-projected coherent state

For `K_z=sum_m z^m/sqrt(m!) e_m` and `z_j=sqrt(n)a_j`, the total-number-n
projection in three modes has squared norm

`sum_{i+j+k=n} n^n a_1^(2i)a_2^(2j)a_3^(2k)/(i!j!k!) = n^n/n!`.

After normalization its `(i,j,k)` coordinate is
`sqrt(n!/(i!j!k!)) a_1^i a_2^j a_3^k`, exactly the coefficient in the
Hermite addition formula.  Parity sectors are the character decomposition of
the three commuting parity operators `J e_m=(-1)^m e_m` restricted to the
number-n space; the four-sector Hadamard formula is therefore exact.
