# R19 decision

`D(r)-1/3 = 1/2 (r C(r)^2)'` is falsified at the first new coefficient in
the known characteristic-zero Q-moment quotient:

```text
d4 - d4_star = (2/3) c2^2
```

Decision: stop searching for this scalar all-orders closure. This does not
disprove Gaussian rigidity and is not an actual probability counterexample.
It identifies the missing ingredient: an independent all-orders
cross-parameter/spatial coherence identity, or a different positive
functional that controls the `c2` direction.
