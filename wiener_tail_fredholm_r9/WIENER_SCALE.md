# Ordinary Wiener scale

For `0<R<infinity`, let `W_R={f(z)=sum c_n z^n: ||f||_R=sum |c_n|R^n<infinity}`.
If `0<r<R`, the inclusion `W_R -> W_r` is compact and its degree-`N` tail
has norm at most `(r/R)^(N+1)`. Entire functions with finite Wiener norm at
every finite radius belong to all `W_R`; this is distinct from arbitrary
`ell^2` membership.
