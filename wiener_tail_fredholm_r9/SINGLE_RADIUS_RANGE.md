# Single-radius range conditions

If the Stage-7 principal part is established on one Wiener space `W_r`,
choose `r` with no zero of `D` on `|z|=r` and factor `D=P_r H_r`, where `P_r`
contains exactly the interior zeros. The zero-free factor is invertible in
`W_r`; range conditions come from `P_r`, namely derivative vanishing below
each interior zero multiplicity. The exact radius condition for evaluation
and derivative functionals is `|zeta|<r`.
