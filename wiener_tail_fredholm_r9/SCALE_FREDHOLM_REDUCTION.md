# R9 decision ledger

The certified part is factorial conjugation, ordinary Wiener tail control,
the finite central-binomial benchmark, and exact simple/multiple-zero
division. The unresolved part is the actual Stage-7 one-high recurrence in
ordinary coefficients with a uniform `m` tail. The package therefore chooses
`NORMALIZATION_REPAIRED_BUT_SYMBOL_LIMIT_NOT_UNIFORM`.
