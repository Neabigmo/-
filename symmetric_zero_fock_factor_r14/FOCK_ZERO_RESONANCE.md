# Fock zero resonance

R13 reduces the exact Fredholm exception to a nonzero symmetric pair of zeros.
At a proposed reduced resonance one may encounter the exact algebraic data

```text
B_H(z_0) = -1/8,
z_0 B_H'(z_0) = 3/4.
```

With the normalized factor `P_a(z)=1-z^2/a^2`, the full Fock identity implies
these equations, rather than merely being consistent formal data.  At
`z_0^2=2a^2`, the first term and its first derivative vanish, while the second
term gives

```text
-8 B_H(z_0) = 1,
6 B_H(z_0) + z_0 B_H'(z_0) = 0.
```

Thus `B_H(z_0)=-1/8` and `z_0 B_H'(z_0)=3/4` are exact consequences of the
factorized Fock identity.  The audit still does not claim that probability
positivity contradicts this complex resonance.

The unresolved bridge is therefore specific: derive an additional nonlinear
constraint from the full Fock/angular identity and probability realizability
that rules out this complex resonance.  MGF positivity on the real axis,
OU dilation, and generic positive-definiteness do not supply that constraint
automatically.
