# Fock zero resonance

R13 reduces the exact Fredholm exception to a nonzero symmetric pair of zeros.
At a proposed reduced resonance one may encounter the exact algebraic data

```text
B_H(z_0) = -1/8,
z_0 B_H'(z_0) = 3/4.
```

The two equations are mutually consistent as formal local data.  The R14
audit checks them symbolically but does not claim that a genuine probability
solution realizes them or that probability positivity contradicts them.

The unresolved bridge is therefore specific: derive an additional nonlinear
constraint from the full Fock/angular identity and probability realizability
that rules out this complex resonance.  MGF positivity on the real axis,
OU dilation, and generic positive-definiteness do not supply that constraint
automatically.
