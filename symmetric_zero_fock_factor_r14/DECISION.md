# R14 decision

`FOCK_ZERO_RESONANCE_LEMMA_CERTIFIED_PROBABILITY_BRIDGE_REMAINS`

Exact replays certify the symmetric probability countermodels, OU zero
transport, the angular elementary identities, the formal resonance data, and
the conjugate-quartet factor.  They do not provide the missing nonlinear
probability/Fock lemma.  The Fredholm/common-zero line should stop here unless
such a lemma is independently supplied.

This is not Gaussian rigidity and does not claim that the full Fock system
admits either countermodel.
