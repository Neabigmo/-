# Minimal-zero audit

A smallest-modulus zero argument is insufficient by itself.  It gives a
modulus constraint on a zero of an analytic function, but the exceptional
pair can lie at a non-real argument and the MGF has no general theorem that
turns minimal modulus into a zero-free disk covering every relevant angular
direction.

The exact `cosh` and three-point examples in this package make that limitation
concrete.  No maximum-modulus numerical experiment is used.
