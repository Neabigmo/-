# Conjugate quartet factor

Let (a=x+i y), with (x,y) real.  For a real-coefficient analytic function,
the symmetric pair and its conjugate give

```text
(z-a)(z+a)(z-conj(a))(z+conj(a))
  = (z^2-a^2)(z^2-conj(a)^2)
  = z^4 - 2*Re(a^2)*z^2 + |a|^4.
```

The factor is even and has real coefficients.  This identity is exact, but
it is only a factor identity; it does not imply infeasibility of the
corresponding probability law.
