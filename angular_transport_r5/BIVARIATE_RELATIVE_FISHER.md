# Bivariate relative-Fisher analogue

For the full pair, define `Pi_y(theta)=g_theta(y)/(2*pi*gamma_2(y))` and
`H2(y)=E_Pi ||grad log g_theta(y)+y||^2`. The relative-Fisher gap is the
Gaussian convolution of `H2` around `(z,0)`. This is an exact cross-angle
formulation, but it does not establish equality.
