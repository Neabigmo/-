# Bivariate tilted mixture

With `a_j=sqrt(2/3) cos(theta+2*pi*(j-1)/3)` and `b_j=a'_j`, exact D3
identities give `Q3=L_theta^2+T_theta^2`. Uniform theta makes the pair
rotation-invariant. Under `Q3~chi^2_2`, the pair is `N((0,0),I2)`; tilting by
`exp(z L-z^2/2)` gives `N((z,0),I2)`.

The pair law is conditional on the target angular identity; no fixed-angle
Gaussian claim is made.
