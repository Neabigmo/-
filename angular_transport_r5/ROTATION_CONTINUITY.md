# Rotation continuity equation

For compactly supported test functions, `L'_theta=T_theta` and
`T'_theta=-L_theta` imply `d_theta int f(x)q_theta(x)dx =
int f'(x)q_theta(x)m_theta(x)dx`. Weak integration by parts yields
`partial_theta q + partial_x(q m)=0`. The joint law obeys the corresponding
Liouville equation. No pointwise density differentiation is assumed.
