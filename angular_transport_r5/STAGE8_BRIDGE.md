# Stage8 handoff

After the bounded Fisher closure fails, the next route is the critical
Hellinger/parity continuum normal form with backward-heat positivity. For the
first nonzero odd cumulant `kappa_d`, the OU-smoothed formal bridge is
`2*pi*pi_x = 1 + rho^d*kappa_d*pbar_d(theta)*H_d(x)/d! + ...`, where
`pbar_d=sum_j cos(theta+phase_j)^d` is unscaled.
The posterior missing-information field has a positive leading term
`rho^(2d)*kappa_d^2*mean(p_d^2)*H_(d-1)(x)^2/((d-1)!)^2`. This is a local bridge,
not a global theorem.
