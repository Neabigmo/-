`TAIL_LOCALITY_GAP`

The dual annihilator characterization, tail-normalization selection, finite
triangular/diagonal replays, and the conditional simultaneous-recurrence to
common-root argument are documented and checked.  The exact obstruction is
not yet certified because the R11 compactness theorem does not imply the
relative high-column tail estimate required after division by `b_i`.

No numerical determinant search, zero search, remote computation, or Gaussian
rigidity claim is justified by this package.
