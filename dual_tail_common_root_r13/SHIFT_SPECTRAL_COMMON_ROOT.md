# Shift spectral common root

Let `(S psi)_p=psi_{p+1}` and let
`V=closure(span{S^p psi:p>=0})` in `ell^infinity`.  If the simultaneous
recurrences hold, the operator power series give

```text
Dtilde_R(S)|_V=0,
Ctilde_R(S)|_V=0.
```

Analytic spectral mapping then implies that every `lambda` in the nonempty
spectrum of `S|V` is a common zero of `Dtilde_R` and `Ctilde_R`; the contraction
bound puts the spectrum in the closed unit disk.  This step is valid only once
the simultaneous recurrences have been obtained.
