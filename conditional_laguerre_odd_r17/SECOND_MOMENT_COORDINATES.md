# Conditional second-moment coordinates

Let

\[
b(T)=\mathbb E[\bar X^2\mid T]=\sum_{n\ge0}d_nL_n(T).
\]

The exact first coefficients are

\[
d_0=\frac13,\qquad d_1=-\frac{\mu_4-3}{9},
\]

\[
d_2=\frac{2\mu_3^2-12\mu_4+\mu_6+21}{54},
\]

\[
d_3=\frac{61\mu_3^2-4\mu_3\mu_5+2\mu_4^2-177\mu_4+25\mu_6-\mu_8+243}{486}.
\]

After the target substitutions,

\[
d_0=\frac13,\quad d_1=0,\quad d_2=\frac32c_1^2,\quad d_3=4c_1c_2.
\]
