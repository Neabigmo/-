# General triangularity

For every tested `n`, the coefficient of the highest odd raw moment `mu_(2n+1)` in

\[
c_n=\mathbb E[\bar X L_n(Q/2)]
\]

is

\[
\frac{(-1)^n}{3^n n!}.
\]

The replay checks this exactly for `n=1,...,6`.  The relation is triangular in the odd moments;
it is the algebraic reason that `m(T) equiv 0` removes the entire odd moment sequence under the
project's existing moment-determinacy assumptions.  It is not, by itself, a proof that
`m(T)` vanishes.
