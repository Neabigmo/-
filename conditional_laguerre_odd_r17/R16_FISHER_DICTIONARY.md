# R16 Fisher dictionary

R16 established the exact dictionary (with `d=1-q`) between the Gaussian-observation law and
the negatively tilted triple law:

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\nu_q}(X)=\frac q3+q^2\operatorname{Var}_{\nu_q}(K_q'),
\]

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{P_q}(\bar X)=\frac13+\operatorname{Var}_{\nu_q}(K_q').
\]

Thus equality in the Gaussian lower bound is exactly the vanishing Fisher deficit. R17 treats
this as a dictionary, not as an independent proof of `m(T)=0`.
