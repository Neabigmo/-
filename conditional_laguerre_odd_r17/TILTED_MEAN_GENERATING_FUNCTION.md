# Tilted mean and Laguerre coefficients

For `T=Q/2`, the coefficient of `L_n` in `m(T)=E[bar X|T]` is

\[
c_n=\mathbb E[\bar X L_n(Q/2)].
\]

The first coefficients are

\[
c_1=-\frac{\mu_3}{3},\qquad
c_2=\frac{\mu_5-10\mu_3}{18},
\]

\[
c_3=-\frac{\mu_7-21\mu_5+105\mu_3}{162},
\]

\[
c_4=\frac{\mu_9-36\mu_7+378\mu_5-1260\mu_3-100\mu_3^3}{1944}.
\]

The fourth coefficient is where the first nonlinear Fock coupling appears.  These formulas are
exact polynomial identities under the target moment relations

\[
\mu_4=3,\quad \mu_6=15+7\mu_3^2,\quad
\mu_8=105-124\mu_3^2+32\mu_3\mu_5.
\]
