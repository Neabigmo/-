# Conditional-variance positivity

The realizability constraint is the pointwise inequality

\[
h(t)=b(t)-m(t)^2=\operatorname{Var}(\bar X\mid T=t)\ge0.
\]

It is stronger than the coefficient equalities `d_0,...,d_3`, but it is not equivalent to a
finite list of coefficient inequalities. The product linearization of Laguerre polynomials
allows the coefficients of `m^2` to be computed exactly; positivity of `h` then produces an
infinite family of quadratic-form inequalities. R17 deliberately stops before pretending that
a finite first-mode argument closes this family.
