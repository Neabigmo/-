# Reparametrization audit

The conditional-Q Laguerre coordinates are a useful probabilistic realization of the odd Fock
sector: they do not introduce a new independent family of constraints merely by changing basis.
What is new is the explicit nonnegative function `Var(bar X|T)`, which couples all Laguerre modes.

The open theorem question is whether the full cross-`x` exponential-family coherence upgrades
this infinite positivity constraint to `m(T)=0`. Pointwise finite-order moment positivity alone
does not do so.
