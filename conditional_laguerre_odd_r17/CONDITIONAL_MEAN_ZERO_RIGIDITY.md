# Conditional-mean-zero implication

If `m(T)=E[bar X|T]` vanishes in `L^2(Exp(1))`, Laguerre completeness gives `c_n=0` for every
`n`. General triangularity then gives all odd moments equal to zero. The stronger conclusion
`R=1` is conditional on the project's existing even-sector/Fock closure and moment-determinacy
hypotheses; it is not re-proved by this local audit.

The converse direction is the useful bridge for later work: proving `m(T)=0` is enough to remove
the odd sector, after which the already established even constraints can be invoked.
