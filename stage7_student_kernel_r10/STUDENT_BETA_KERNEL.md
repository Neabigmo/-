# Student/Beta kernel

For `a=-1/2-sqrt(3)T/2` and `b=-1/2+sqrt(3)T/2`, the exact endpoint kernel is
`A_(n-m,j,k)/A_n00 = E_n[a^j b^k]`, with the expectation evaluated by exact
polynomial expansion and Student/Beta moments. No floating-point quadrature
or asymptotic replacement is used in the replay.
