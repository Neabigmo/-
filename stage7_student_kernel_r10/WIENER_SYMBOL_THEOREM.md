# Wiener symbol theorem status

On ordinary Wiener spaces, a bound `C sqrt(n+1)` together with `r/R<1`
produces tails bounded by `C sup_{n>=2N}sqrt(n+1)(r/R)^n`, which tend to zero.
The finite-band Student limits identify the candidate symbol
`D_R(z)=[R(z/2)^2+R(-z/2)^2]/2`.

R10 stops short of claiming the full theorem-level operator identity because
the original Stage-7 kernel and a uniform-in-band proof are not in this
workspace.
